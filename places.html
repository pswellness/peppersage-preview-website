Destinations

Destinations

Our favourite places in and around Chengdu, and further into the mountains. Every journey can be built around them.

Six favourites

Chengdu Panda Base

Half day · 30 min from the city

Around 200 giant and red pandas in bamboo forest. We arrive at 7:30 opening, when they feed and play.

Sanxingdui Museum

Full day · 1 h north

Bronze masks and a four-metre bronze tree from a 3,000-year-old civilisation with no written record.

Mount Qingcheng

1–2 days · 1 h 15 min west

A birthplace of Taoism: temples in cypress forest, dawn qigong and a forest resort under 1,300 m.

Dujiangyan Irrigation System

Half day · 1 h west

A UNESCO waterworks from 256 BC that still waters the Chengdu plain, on the road to Qingcheng.

Leshan Giant Buddha

Full day · 1 h by train

The world’s largest stone Buddha, 71 m tall, seen from the river or the cliff path.

Siguniang Mountains

2–3 days · 3,200 m

Four snow peaks above valleys of larch and meadow, with boardwalks that keep walking gentle.

Chengdu and day trips

Jinsha Site Museum

2–3 hours · in the city

The ancient Shu capital, home of the gold Sun and Immortal Birds disc.

Sichuan Museum

2–3 hours · in the city

Han-dynasty storyteller figures, Tibetan Buddhist art and Zhang Daqian’s paintings.

Wenshu Monastery

2 hours · city centre

A Tang-dynasty Zen temple with a teahouse and a much-loved vegetarian lunch.

Kuanzhai Alley

2 hours · city centre

Three Qing-dynasty lanes of courtyard houses, teahouses and crafts, best at dusk.

People’s Park teahouse

2 hours · city centre

Covered-bowl tea in a bamboo chair while the city dances and practises tai chi.

Wuhou Shrine and Jinli

Half day · 15 min south

A Three Kingdoms temple beside lantern-lit lanes of snacks and crafts.

Du Fu Thatched Cottage

2 hours · 20 min west

The quietest garden in the city, where the Tang poet wrote over 240 poems.

Taikoo Li and Daci Temple

2–3 hours · city centre

Design shops and cafés around an ancient temple, with a Michelin-starred vegetarian teahouse.

Sichuan Opera

Evening · city centre

Face-changing, fire-spitting and shadow puppets. No translation needed.

Sichuan Cuisine Museum

Half day · 45 min west

A garden museum with hands-on cooking classes, adaptable for low-salt diets.

Huanglongxi Ancient Town

Half day · 1 h south

A riverside town of stilt houses, banyan trees and stone bridges.

Mountains and beyond

Always after your screening, never before.

Danba

2 days · 1,900 m

Jiarong Tibetan villages of painted stone houses and centuries-old watchtowers.

Hailuogou

2 days · glacier viewpoint ~3,000 m

A glacier flowing into forest below Mount Gongga, with natural hot springs.

Mount Mengding

1 day · 1.5 h south-west

The mountain where tea was first cultivated, with a tea master.

Mount Emei

1–2 days · summit 3,079 m

A sacred Buddhist mountain with temples, forest trails and sunrise over the clouds.

Jiuzhaigou and Huanglong

3 days · up to 3,500 m

Turquoise lakes, waterfalls and travertine terraces in two UNESCO valleys.

Daocheng Yading

4–5 days · up to 4,600 m

Three sacred snow peaks and glacial lakes, only after an altitude check.

Bifengxia Panda Base

1 day · 2 h south-west

A quiet panda base in a forested gorge with a volunteer keeper day.
