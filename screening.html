Health screening

Health screening, tailored to you

Three levels give you a starting point. Our doctor then adjusts your list with add-ons and swaps, so you test for what matters to you, and nothing you don’t need.

Choose a starting level

Level 1

Essential

68 tests

A thorough baseline for adults 35 and over.

  • Chest CT with coronary calcium score
  • Echocardiogram, carotid ultrasound and 2-hour heart monitor
  • Capsule endoscopy of stomach and bowel
  • One advanced scan of your choice: brain, neck or lower-back MRI
Level 2

In-Depth

86 tests

For 45 and over, or a family history of heart disease, stroke or cancer.

  • Brain MRI with brain-vessel MRA
  • Inflammation, immunity and thyroid antibodies
  • Hepatitis A and C, liver and kidney early-damage markers
  • Genetic risk reports and a mental-health assessment
Level 3

Complete

95 tests

Our fullest picture, for 55 and over or anyone who wants nothing left out.

  • Everything in In-Depth
  • Neck-artery MRA and insulin
  • Autoimmune and complement panels, HIV and syphilis
  • Capsule endoscopy of stomach and bowel
